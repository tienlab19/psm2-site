FAQ

Is any content locked?

No. All 9 sets and 180 questions are available from the first launch. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The question bank ships inside the app and never needs a network connection.

Where is my progress stored?

On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it cannot be recovered.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings inside the app and choose Reset progress.

How is the mock exam scored?

30 questions in 90 minutes. We use an 85% practice threshold, deliberately stricter than the real 85% pass mark so you have margin on exam day.

I found a wrong answer or explanation.

Please email the question text or a screenshot. Content corrections ship in the next update.

Which iOS versions are supported?

iOS 17.0 and later.

Report a bug

Include your iOS version, device model, app version and the steps to reproduce. That is usually enough for a fix.
