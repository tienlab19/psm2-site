Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

PSM II Prep ("the app") is built to work entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit or sell your personal data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account system, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK. It does not send your usage, answers or identifiers to us or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

  • Quiz progress, scores and session state
  • Bookmarked, skipped and incorrectly answered questions
  • Your planned exam date and app settings such as language

This data is saved locally using iOS UserDefaults. It never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the app permanently deletes this data.

Network access

The question bank ships inside the app, so the app functions fully offline. Links you tap that open Safari (for example the App Store or an email link) are handled by Apple and by the destination site under their own policies.

Purchases

The app is free and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. If purchases are introduced in a future version, transactions are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase. You control all app data directly on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
